# Israeli vehicle-history / car-data reports: market size, demand and willingness to pay

Date checked for all sources: 2026-11-25. Confidence labels: **[primary]** = verified in primary source; **[secondary]** = secondary source (press or search snippet, not opened in full); **[estimate]** = my estimate.

**Method caveat (important for the report writer):** In this session WebFetch could not reach most Israeli sites (calcalist.co.il, auto.co.il, icar.co.il, carzone.co.il, ynetnews.com all failed DNS resolution under the session's network allowlist). So nearly every Israeli figure below comes from search-engine snippets of the articles, not from reading the full article. No figure here is "verified in primary source". To verify, someone needs to open the Ministry of Transport (data.gov.il) transfer tables and the CBS "Motor Vehicles 2025" publication directly.

## Q1. Used-car ownership transfers (העברות בעלות) per year, and the split by channel

### Takeaway
Israel records about **1.27–1.32 million used-car ownership transfers a year** (2024–2025, Ministry of Transport data as reported by Calcalist). This counts transfer *events*, not buyers. A sale through a dealer creates two transfers (seller to dealer, dealer to buyer), so the number of end-buyer purchases is probably around **0.7–1.0 million a year** [estimate]. I found no published split between private-to-private, dealer and leasing transfers. The sources disagree on the exact 2025 figure.

### Cited Findings
- 2025: **1,316,302** used-car ownership transfers, against **1,293,862** in 2024 (+1.7%), per Ministry of Transport data. **[secondary]** — [Calcalist (search snippet)](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/car/article/sjwlh3hhzg)
- **Contradiction:** a second search summary of the same Calcalist article gave **1,293,862 for 2025** (+1.7% year on year). That is the same number the first summary gave for 2024, so one of the two summaries mislabels the year. Most likely reading: 2024 ≈ 1.29M and 2025 ≈ 1.32M. — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/car/article/sjwlh3hhzg)
- H1 2025: **636,971** vehicle ownership transfers. The share of used cars fell slightly while transfers of brand-new "0 km" cars rose. **[secondary]** — [Calcalist, "יותר אפס קילומטר, פחות עסקאות: כך נראה שוק המשומשות ב-2025"](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/car/article/bypctuqlxx). This annualises to about 1.27M, which fits the full-year figure.
- 2025 transfers of model-year-2025 cars: **64,342** for the full year and **20,715** in H1. Calcalist reads these as importer-to-leasing or importer-to-dealer "יד ראשונה אפס ק"מ" cars. **[secondary]** — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/car/article/sjwlh3hhzg); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/car/article/bypctuqlxx)
- Used EV transfers: **over 51,000 in 2025**, against about 33,000 in 2024. **[secondary]** — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/car/article/sjwlh3hhzg)
- A Ken Research page cites **714,007** vehicle ownership transfers for 2024. A ynet item reports that about **714,000** vehicles changed ownership in 2023. **Contradiction** with the ~1.3M series. The 714k figure probably counts unique vehicles or a different definition, but I could not confirm which. **[secondary]** — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/israel-car-finance-leasing-market.md); [ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/culture/article/bjxp11urkee)
- The Ministry of Transport publishes a free open dataset of each private car's ownership history by quarter, with ownership type (private, company, leasing, rental, dealer). From this the channel split could be computed. **[secondary: dataset page exists]** — [gov.il מאגר היסטוריית כלי רכב פרטיים (2)](https://www.gov.il/he/Departments/DynamicCollectors/private_vehicle_history_2)
- The gov.il transfer service covers cars registered to individuals, companies, dealers or partnerships, and has a separate dealer-to-private route. **[secondary]** — [gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/service/ownership-vehicles-transfer); [gov.il (Arabic, dealer to private)](https://www.gov.il/ar/service/registering-change-ownership-merchant-to-private)

### Inferences
- [estimate] Unique end-buyer used-car purchases per year: about **0.7–1.0M**. Assumptions: (a) 30–50% of transfers are intermediate dealer or trade-in legs; (b) the 714k figure may be the unique-vehicle count, which would put the floor near 0.7M.
- [estimate] Report-buying occasions (a buyer seriously evaluating a specific car): often 1–3 candidate cars checked per purchase, so roughly **1–3M "lookup occasions" a year** at the top of the funnel.

### Gaps
- No published split between private-to-private, dealer and leasing/rental-origin transfers. It can be derived from the data.gov.il ownership-history dataset; I did not compute it.
- No 2026 year-to-date transfer count found.
- The CBS (הלמ"ס) Motor Vehicles report counts ownership changes, but I could not reach the table. The 2024 edition (published Nov 2025) is listed at [cbs.gov.il](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/DocLib/2025/1978/e_print.pdf).

## Q2. Fleet size, new-car sales, leasing and rental share entering the used market

### Takeaway
At end-2025 the fleet was about **4.35M motor vehicles, of which about 3.79M were private cars**, with an average age of about 7.8 years. New-car deliveries hit a **record ~293.6k in 2025** and about **177k in H1 2026** (+10.7%). Roughly **46% of new cars are registered to companies** (leasing, rental, fleets), and these return to the used market after about 2–4 years.

### Cited Findings
- End-2025 fleet: **4.35M** motor vehicles (+3% vs 2024), of which **3.789M** private cars. EVs: **216,265** (+33%). **[secondary, citing CBS]** — [Walla](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3836236); [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/israel/article-1378411)
- Average vehicle age: **7.8 years** at end-2025 (7.6 in 2024, 7.5 in 2023). **[secondary]** — [icar](https://icar.co.il/news/rjuyxulrbx)
- Total distance driven: 69.3 billion km in 2025 (+2.6%). **[secondary]** — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/israel/article-1378411)
- 2025 new-vehicle deliveries: **293,591**, a record (+7.4% vs 2024; previous record 286,728 in 2016). Chinese brands had about 35% share. **[secondary, citing importers' data]** — [auto.co.il](https://www.auto.co.il/articles/car-news/local-news/cars-sales-2025/); [Cartube](https://www.cartube.co.il/%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91/%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%90-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%9B%D7%9C-%D7%94%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91-%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C-%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9D-2025)
- H1 2026: about **177,000** deliveries (+10.7%), Chinese brands about 45%. **[secondary]** — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/additives/245/27CAR.html)
- H1 2025: 145,834 deliveries, of which **46% were registered to companies** and only **54% to private persons**. Many went to leasing companies and dealer groups that resell them quickly as "יד ראשונה אפס ק"מ". Some "private" registrations are actually employees of companies (a workaround for buyer-discount rules), so the private share is overstated. **[secondary]** — [Calcalist "46% מהרכבים החדשים מגיעים לציי רכב"](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/car/article/bjt17bddll)
- 2026 is on track for record scrappage: over 82k private cars permanently taken off the road year to date. **[secondary]** — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/consumerism/article-1390174)
- The Tax Authority's 2025 vehicle-sector review says used-car prices kept falling relative to new-car prices and the CPI. **[secondary: PDF located, not read]** — [gov.il Tax Authority review 2025](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/tax-reviewvehicle/he/Publications_VehicleReview_vehicle2025-1.pdf)
- About **3,000** new cars were sold by leasing companies as 0-km cars last year. **[secondary]** — [bhol](https://www.bhol.co.il/news/147267)

### Inferences
- [estimate] Leasing and rental-origin inflow to the used market: about 46% of ~270–295k new cars a year ≈ **120–135k fleet cars a year**, returning after 2–4 years. These cars are a natural target for history reports ("was it a rental car?", "מקוריות", ex-leasing stigma, "כתם הליסינג" — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local/articles/0,7340,L-3634199,00.html)).
- [estimate] Transfers ≈ 1.3M against 3.79M private cars, about 0.34 transfers per car per year. Even if intermediate dealer legs are excluded, the average private car changes hands every ~4–5 years.

### Gaps
- No published share of used-market supply that is ex-leasing or ex-rental. Derivable from the ownership-type field in the data.gov.il history dataset.

## Q3. What buyers pay today for inspection, price lookup or reports

### Takeaway
Paid pre-purchase inspection (בדיקה לפני קנייה, Ministry of Transport procedure 127) is the established paid habit, at **₪300–1,000** (luxury up to ₪1,500). Basic registry data is **free** from gov.il open datasets, and the insurance clearing house's claims-history report costs about **₪12**. Carzone sells report packages, but I could not retrieve its price. **No survey was found** on the share of buyers who pay for inspection or a report.

### Cited Findings
- Garage inspection ₪300–500 for a regular car. An authorised institute (מכון) costs about ₪200–500 more. **[secondary]** — [Midrag](https://www.midrag.co.il/pages/NCR/Content/Price/10949)
- Standard private car ₪350–700; luxury ₪750–1,500. **[secondary]** — [d.co.il price list](https://www.d.co.il/priceList-28290/). Another d.co.il page says ₪450–600. — [d.co.il](https://www.d.co.il/Article1579/)
- A comprehensive institute check costs from a few hundred shekels up to about ₪1,000. **[secondary]** — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/car/news/article/20035337)
- Inspection follows MoT procedure 127. It is a general check without computerised systems, so it gives a partial picture for modern or electric cars. Licensed inspectors are "מקצוע 180". **[secondary]** — [auto.co.il](https://www.auto.co.il/articles/car-news/local-news/used-car-inspection/)
- Industry voices (Techno Test) call the purchase inspection "a must, not a luxury", especially EV battery checks, and warn of odometer fraud detectable via the gearbox computer. **[secondary, opinion]** — [icar](https://icar.co.il/magazine_9.25/sybitpkjel)
- Free government data: MoT datasets give, by plate, detailed vehicle info (active private cars since 2017, mileage at test, structural changes) and the number and type of past ownerships by quarter. **[secondary]** — [gov.il history (1)](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/dynamiccollectors/private_vehicle_history_1); [gov.il history (2)](https://www.gov.il/he/Departments/DynamicCollectors/private_vehicle_history_2). The government's shared price list (מחירון) of the Ministry of Transport is also free. — [protocol.co.il](https://protocol.co.il/vehicle-price-list/). A personal ownership report for owners is available in the gov.il personal area. — [gov.il](https://www.gov.il/he/service/vehicle-ownership-report)
- Carzone (קארזון) report: ownership history, originality, past ads with photos, test reports, mileage including tampering suspicion, and the price list. Sold in **packages** valid for 12 months, with **2 free reports on sign-up**. MoT does not publish all extended data for cars over about 8 years old. It does **not** include insurance-claims history; for that Carzone points users to the insurance clearing house report, which costs **₪12**. **[secondary]** — [carzone car-report](https://www.carzone.co.il/car-report); [carzone order cancellation](https://www.carzone.co.il/order-cancellation); [carzone slika](https://www.carzone.co.il/slika)
- Cartube offers a free plate lookup (model, ownership history, test mileage, open recalls, safety equipment). **[secondary]** — [Cartube](https://www.cartube.co.il/איתור-מספר-רכב)
- Related transaction costs a buyer already faces: lien release from a bank or leasing company about ₪170–250. **[secondary]** — [container.org.il](https://www.container.org.il/%D7%9B%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91/)

### Inferences
- [estimate] Price anchors for a private buyer: free (gov.il, Cartube) → ₪12 (clearing-house claims report) → a paid aggregated report (Carzone; price not found, likely tens of shekels) → ₪300–1,000 physical inspection. A digital report priced at **₪20–80** sits well below inspection and above free data. The value proposition must beat the free government datasets, for example by adding accident/claims data, odometer-fraud flags, ad history and market price.

### Gaps
- **Not found:** any survey on the share of Israeli used-car buyers who pay for inspection, a report or a price list.
- **Not found:** Carzone's current per-report or package price, and the Levi Itzhak (לוי יצחק) price-list subscription price for consumers or dealers.
- **Not found:** number of inspections performed per year in Israel.

## Q4. Willingness to pay by segment and international reference prices

### Takeaway
Foreign benchmarks: **Carfax US $44.99 for one report, $64.99 for three, $99.99 for six**; **UK HPI full check £19.99**; carVertical roughly **€13–35 per report**, varying by country (weak evidence). I found no Israeli WTP data by segment (private buyer, dealer, garage, leasing company, insurer).

### Cited Findings
- Carfax: $44.99 for 1, $64.99 for 3, $99.99 for 6. Reports valid 60 days. Prices verified by the source on 2026-09-30. **[secondary]** — [carwhere.com](https://www.carwhere.com/guides/carfax-report-cost); [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/carfax)
- HPI (UK): full check from £19.99, plus £1.75 for a postal copy; a cheaper basic check exists. Described as the most expensive of the UK checks compared. Several of these articles are affiliate content. **[secondary]** — [Auto Express](https://www.autoexpress.co.uk/accessories-tyres/43192/hpi-check); [caranalytics](https://www.caranalytics.co.uk/guides/who-has-the-cheapest-hpi-check/)
- carVertical: "from $12.79 per report" (sponsored article). UK reviews mention about £35; a Dutch review mentions about €30. The official pricing pages load prices dynamically, so I could not read them. **Low confidence.** — [muddyrivernews (sponsored)](https://muddyrivernews.com/sponsored-content/3-best-tools-to-use-before-buying-a-used-car-in-2026/20260430074652/); [carVertical pricing](https://www.carvertical.com/en/pricing)
- Low-cost US resellers advertise Carfax-type reports at about $5.99. These are promotional posts and show price pressure at the bottom of the market. **[secondary, promotional]** — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/low-cost-carfax-why-5-99-is-the-right-price-for-vehicle-history-in-2026-trKQIVRr7j0Ef4mZ6lTt)

### Inferences
- [estimate] Converted at about ₪3.6–3.7/$ and ₪4.6–4.7/£, the benchmarks are Carfax ≈ ₪165, HPI ≈ ₪92 and carVertical ≈ ₪45–130. Israel has much more free government data than the US, so willingness to pay is likely lower. A private one-off buyer's range of **₪20–80 per report** is my estimate, not a measured figure.
- [estimate] By segment (no Israeli evidence; reasoning only):
  - Private one-time buyer: per-report or small bundle (2–5 plates), ₪20–80 per report.
  - Small dealer (סוחר רכב): volume pricing. Carzone's package model suggests demand exists; perhaps ₪5–20 per report or a monthly subscription.
  - Garage (מוסך) or inspection institute: an embedded or upsell tool.
  - Leasing companies and insurers: these are data *suppliers* as much as customers, and they buy via API or enterprise contracts. Insurers already have the clearing house (מסלקה).

### Gaps
- **Not found:** any Israeli segment-level WTP data, dealer subscription prices, or B2B API prices.

## Q6. Seasonality and purchase frequency

### Takeaway
The need is mostly **one-off for private buyers**, who buy a car every few years. It is **recurring for dealers, garages and fleets**. I found no Israeli data on purchase frequency per household or on used-market seasonality.

### Cited Findings
- New-car deliveries are strongly seasonal. January is typically high because of the registration-year effect and tax/green-tax changes, and December 2025 had only 10,004 deliveries. **[secondary]** — [Cartube 2025 summary](https://www.cartube.co.il/%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91/%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%90-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%9B%D7%9C-%D7%94%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91-%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C-%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9D-2025); [auto.co.il Jan 2026](https://www.auto.co.il/articles/car-news/local-news/car-sales-jan-2026/)
- The average vehicle age of 7.8 years means a large stock of older cars, where MoT extended data is weakest (over about 8 years old). **[secondary]** — [icar](https://icar.co.il/news/rjuyxulrbx); [carzone](https://www.carzone.co.il/car-report)

### Inferences
- [estimate] With about 3.79M private cars and about 0.7–1.0M unique used purchases a year, a typical private owner buys a used car roughly every **4–6 years**. So consumer revenue depends on reaching buyers at the moment of purchase (SEO, classifieds integration) rather than on subscriptions. Recurring revenue lies with dealers, garages and fleets.
- [estimate] Used-car seasonality probably follows the new-car cycle: a trade-in wave after January/early-year deliveries and the "0 km" leasing resale flow. Not verified.

### Gaps
- **Not found:** monthly used-transfer seasonality, household purchase frequency, and holding-period statistics for Israel.
