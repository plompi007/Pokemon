## 1. Carzone (carzone.co.il) — offer, price, owner, data sources, terms

### Takeaway
Carzone sells a plate-number report ("דו"ח קארזון") with a free preview and a paid full report. It covers ownership history, originality, past sale listings with photos, test-station odometer readings with tampering flags, and a per-plate price estimate. It explicitly does NOT include insurance-claims/accident history, and it points users to the insurers' clearing-center report (~12 ₪) as an add-on. The exact price of the full report, the owning entity, the founding date and the terms-of-use clauses were **not found**.

### Cited Findings
- The report is generated by license-plate number. A preview can be seen without payment, and the full report is paid. — [carzone.co.il/car-report](https://www.carzone.co.il/car-report) (secondary source: search snippet of primary page)
- Stated contents: ownership history, originality (מקוריות), past listings of the vehicle including photos, test reports (דיווחי טסטים), mileage including tampering ("קילומטראז׳ - כולל זיופים"), and a car price list. Red flags it advertises: suspected odometer fraud, stolen car, many ownership changes, suspected total loss ("אובדן להלכה"). — [carzone.co.il](https://carzone.co.il/), [carzone.co.il/car-report](https://www.carzone.co.il/car-report) (secondary source: search snippets)
- Odometer method: it compares odometer readings from annual licensing tests with readings in past sale listings to flag inconsistencies. It calls itself "the leading tool in Israel" for this (self-claim). — [carzone.co.il/news/kmTampering](https://www.carzone.co.il/news/kmTampering) (secondary source: search snippet)
- Data-source scope statement: mileage and ownership-transfer data from the Ministry of Transport is mainly available for private cars from 2017 onward, and not for commercial vehicles, taxis, buses, motorcycles or trucks. This matches the scope of the gov.il open datasets "מאגר היסטוריית כלי רכב פרטיים (1)/(2)" (private vehicles active since 2017: odometer, color/tire/LPG changes, ownership dates by quarter, ownership type). — [carzone.co.il/news/kmTampering](https://www.carzone.co.il/news/kmTampering) (secondary); [gov.il private_vehicle_history_1](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/dynamiccollectors/private_vehicle_history_1), [gov.il private_vehicle_history_2](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/dynamiccollectors/private_vehicle_history_2) (secondary: search snippets of primary gov pages)
- Price estimate: Carzone gives an estimated price range tailored to the specific car by plate, not just a model-level price. — [carzone.co.il/car-report](https://www.carzone.co.il/car-report) (secondary: search snippet)
- Insurance/accidents: the Carzone report does NOT include an insurance clearing-center check. The clearing report can be added for an extra fee, even after buying the Carzone report. The clearing-center report costs 12 ₪ incl. VAT when bought directly and covers the last 7 years. Leasing/rental cars usually lack comprehensive insurance and are not reported to the clearing center, so a clean record does not guarantee no accidents. — [carzone.co.il/slika](https://www.carzone.co.il/slika) (secondary: search snippet)
- Other site content: new-car price lists and spec pages (e.g. Honda CR-V 2026 at a list price of 320,000 ₪), news articles, a used-car listings section ("bulletins") with login and "new listing". This suggests extra revenue from listings and ads, beyond reports. — [carzone.co.il/Honda/CR-V/2026](https://www.carzone.co.il/Honda/CR-V/2026), [carzone.co.il/bulletins/new](https://www.carzone.co.il/bulletins/new) (secondary: search result titles)
- Price of the full report: NOT FOUND. A 2026 third-party guide that lists Carzone among others gives generic ranges: basic commercial report 0–15 ₪, detailed report with odometer history about 20–40 ₪. These are approximate and not Carzone-specific. — [mcars.co.il/blog/doch-historiyat-rechev](https://mcars.co.il/blog/doch-historiyat-rechev) (secondary source, via search summary)
- Forum users (older threads, about 2–3 years old) say Carzone gives "more details" than competitors, such as past listings for the same car, and that the "first 10 searches are free" (a historical freemium detail, not confirmed for now). One user was skeptical that the estimated value is inflated. — [carsforum.co.il topic 732708 "קארזון, אתר אמין?"](https://carsforum.co.il/topic/732708-%D7%A7%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%96%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%90%D7%AA%D7%A8-%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9F/), [carsforum.co.il topic 739833 "דוח לפני קניה, מי הכי משתלם?"](https://carsforum.co.il/topic/739833-%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%9C%D7%A4%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%94-%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%AA%D7%9C%D7%9D/) (secondary: search summary, not read in full)
- Ownership/operator: NOT FOUND. Searches for the company in Globes, Calcalist and Geektime returned only unrelated companies named "Carzone" (an Irish marketplace bought by Mediahuis for €30M in 2022, and a Chinese auto-parts company). These are NOT the Israeli site. — [Wikipedia: Carzone (Ireland)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carzone) (secondary)
- An "about" page exists at [carzone.co.il/about](https://www.carzone.co.il/about), but its contents (company name, founders, founding year) were not visible in search snippets.

### Inferences
- (my estimate) Carzone's main moat is its **historical archive of used-car listings and photos**, which it collected from its own listings and possibly other sources. That is what lets it check listing odometer readings against test readings. Anyone can rebuild the gov.il part (ownership counts, test odometer, model data, recalls); the listing archive is much harder to copy.
- (my estimate) The likely mix of revenue: paid per-report fees, a clearing-center upsell or referral, ads and content (new-car price lists, news), and used-car listings.
- (my estimate) Its data looks mostly like gov.il open data (data.gov.il vehicle registry, history datasets, recalls, price lists) plus its own listings archive. No licensed insurance, leasing-company or garage data partnership was found.

## 2. Other competitors in Israel — free vs paid, prices, revenue model

### Takeaway
The market splits into tiers. (a) Free gov.il data and free apps or sites built on it. (b) Cheap official paid checks: the clearing center at 10–12 ₪ and the Justice Ministry lien check at 11 ₪. (c) Private plate reports: Carzone, Cartube, Autoboom (about 25–55 ₪ per report), and Find-Car (lien and stolen check at 280 ₪, or 140 ₪ for subscribers). (d) Price lists: Levi Itzhak (paid) and Yad2 (free). (e) Physical pre-purchase inspections at roughly 350–1,000 ₪, and more for luxury cars.

### Cited Findings
**Government (free / official paid)**
- gov.il "מאגר היסטוריית כלי רכב פרטיים (1)": private vehicles active since 2017. Shows odometer reading and whether the color, tires or LPG were changed. Dataset "(2)": ownership dates by quarter and ownership type. Neither covers accidents. — [gov.il (1)](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/dynamiccollectors/private_vehicle_history_1), [gov.il (2)](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/dynamiccollectors/private_vehicle_history_2) (secondary: search snippets of primary page)
- The Ministry of Transport runs a crowdsourced used-car price list based on prices reported at ownership transfer. — [carlistprice.mot.gov.il](https://carlistprice.mot.gov.il/) (secondary: search summary)
- gov.il offers a vehicle-ownership report for current and past registered owners through the personal portal. — [gov.il vehicle-ownership-report](https://www.gov.il/he/service/vehicle-ownership-report) (secondary)
- Justice Ministry lien check (בדיקת משכון כלי רכב): 11 ₪, results by email within seconds. Private companies charge "considerably more" for the same check. — search summary of [cars.walla.co.il/item/3320148](https://cars.walla.co.il/item/3320148) and [gov.il registration_lien_cancellation](https://www.gov.il/he/service/registration_lien_cancellation) (secondary; source of the exact 11 ₪ figure not pinned to one URL, medium confidence)

**Insurance-claims history (מרכז הסליקה של חברות הביטוח)**
- The clearing center database covers insurers' reports on vehicles in accidents over the past 7 years: coverage history, insurer, and property-damage claims paid. Contributing insurers include AIG, Direct, Eylon, Phoenix, Harel, Migdal, Clal, Menora, Shlomo and others. — [icar.co.il news article](https://www.icar.co.il/%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91/%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9_%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2_%D7%A2%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%A8_%D7%91%D7%99%D7%98%D7%95%D7%97%D7%99_%D7%9C%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91_%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A9) (secondary)
- Price: CONTRADICTION / time change. icar and auto.co.il (older, apparently about 2022) say 10 ₪ per check. Carzone's /slika page says 12 ₪ incl. VAT. The 12 ₪ figure is probably current. — [icar](https://www.icar.co.il/%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91/%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9_%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2_%D7%A2%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%A8_%D7%91%D7%99%D7%98%D7%95%D7%97%D7%99_%D7%9C%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91_%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A9), [auto.co.il/article/129901](https://www.auto.co.il/article/129901-local-news); vs [carzone.co.il/slika](https://www.carzone.co.il/slika) (all secondary)
- Access restriction: only the registered owner may get the data (privacy law), so a buyer must ask the seller to pull it and forward it. It also misses accidents that were settled without insurers. — [icar](https://www.icar.co.il/%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91/%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9_%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2_%D7%A2%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%A8_%D7%91%D7%99%D7%98%D7%95%D7%97%D7%99_%D7%9C%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91_%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A9), [auto.co.il](https://www.auto.co.il/article/129901-local-news) (secondary)
- As of 2022, the state had not changed privacy law to allow one unified database of maintenance, accidents and similar records. — [cars.walla.co.il/item/3547735](https://cars.walla.co.il/item/3547735) (secondary; status in 2026 not verified)
- A separate document, the "no-claims" insurance history (העדר תביעות / הר הביטוח), covers up to 3 years and excludes compulsory insurance. — [protocol.co.il](https://protocol.co.il/insurance-claims-history/) (secondary)
- "Shirbit" as a claims-database service: not found. Shirbit is an insurer, not a claims-history service, and no such product surfaced.

**Private plate-report sites**
- Cartube (cartube.co.il): plate lookup with a "full report" covering model details, ownership/cost history, test mileage, open recalls and safety equipment. It is built by cross-referencing Ministry of Transport open data and has no owner identity. Price not found. — [cartube.co.il/איתור-מספר-רכב](https://www.cartube.co.il/איתור-מספר-רכב) (secondary: search snippet)
- Autoboom (autoboom.co.il/check-car): headline "free price list and history" by plate. A paid report has volume pricing: 55 ₪ for 1 car, down to 25 ₪ per report for a 10-pack. — [autoboom.co.il/check-car](https://autoboom.co.il/check-car) (secondary: search summary, medium confidence)
- Find-Car (find-car.co.il): lien and stolen-vehicle report at 280 ₪, or 140 ₪ for site subscribers. That is far above the 11 ₪ official lien fee. — [find-car.co.il/home](https://www.find-car.co.il/home) (secondary: search summary, medium confidence)
- Check-Car (check-car.co.il): free reports from Ministry of Transport data for cars, motorcycles and trucks. — [check-car.co.il](https://www.check-car.co.il/) (secondary)
- "mcars.co.il" publishes 2026 SEO guides on history reports and price lists. It is a content and affiliate competitor for search traffic. — [mcars.co.il](https://mcars.co.il/blog/doch-historiyat-rechev) (secondary)
- TheMarker (June 2023): "New companies offer a pre-purchase condition report for used cars, and prices are falling." The article is paywalled and its content was not read. — [themarker.com 2023-06-08](https://www.themarker.com/dynamo/cars/2023-06-08/ty-article/.premium/00000188-9a39-deaa-ab9d-9ebdbd9d0000) (secondary: title only)
- "רכב דאטה" and "CarCheck" as named Israeli brands: not found. Telegram and WhatsApp plate bots: not found in searches.

Not an official app; disclaims accuracy and warns data may be missing. — [App Store Autoil](https://apps.apple.com/il/app/id6450055352), [Google Play dev Tchelet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=Tchelet) (secondary: search snippet)
- Other plate apps: "פרטי רכב - סטטוס קאר" (ownership, test dates, disabled-parking badge), "חיפוש רכב לפי מספר רישוי" (technical data for vehicles since 1996), PicACar (lookup by photo or plate), CarReader, and My Car (Google Play, 3.2★). — [Status Car](https://apps.apple.com/il/app/%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%99-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91-%D7%A1%D7%98%D7%98%D7%95%D7%A1-%D7%A7%D7%90%D7%A8/id6449218227), [id1554154896](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1554154896), [PicACar](https://apps.apple.com/bz/app/picacar/id6464317633), [CarReader](https://apps.apple.com/il/app/carreader/id6446223989), [Mycar dev page](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=Mycar) (secondary). Download counts and reviews: not found.

**Price lists**
- Levi Itzhak: printed annual subscription at 840 ₪ for 12 months, incl. VAT and shipping. Older online tiers were 10 ₪ per day, 25 ₪ per week and 50 ₪ per month, with a single printed issue at 45 ₪ (older, undated, probably outdated). Mako reported its online price list "now free" (undated). Its model is not based on reported sale prices. — [levi-itzhak.co.il annual subscription](https://levi-itzhak.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%91-%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA%D7%99), [mako](https://www.mako.co.il/cars-cars-news/local/Article-f792d88205b8231006.htm), [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1000613392) (secondary; current online pricing not verified, CONTRADICTION between "free" and paid tiers, which may differ by period)
- Yad2 price list: free online and based on seller-reported sale prices. Levi Itzhak publicly attacked it: "prices in the Yad2 price list are delusional; they failed on reliability." A 2026 comparison says Yad2 prices tend to run higher than Levi Itzhak's for the same car. Revenue model: ads and listings (my estimate). — [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1000613392), [mcars 2026 comparison](https://mcars.co.il/blog/machiron-levi-yitzhak-mul-yad2) (secondary)
- Whether Yad2 sells a paid "car report" in 2026: not found.

**Pre-purchase inspection institutes**
- Price ranges by source: standard private car 450–600 ₪ (one list) or 350–700 ₪ (another); luxury cars 750–2,500 ₪; commercial 600–1,200 ₪; a mechanic charges about 300–500 ₪, and an institute adds about 200–500 ₪ on top; another guide gives 550–900 ₪. The work follows MoT procedure 127 and does not test electronic systems by computer. — [d.co.il priceList-28290](https://www.d.co.il/priceList-28290/), [midrag](https://www.midrag.co.il/Content/Price/10949), [auto.co.il used-car-inspection](https://www.auto.co.il/articles/car-news/local-news/used-car-inspection/), [mcars](https://mcars.co.il/blog/doch-historiyat-rechev) (secondary; years vary)

**Foreign comparables**
- carVertical: $29.99 per report, or $16.99 per report in a 3-pack (global pricing page). No Israel locale or coverage was found. — [carvertical.com/en/pricing](https://www.carvertical.com/en/pricing) (secondary: search snippet). Israel operation: not found.
- Carfax: used in Israel mainly for US-imported cars. A clean Carfax report has historically been required for import approval, at about $40 per report on carfax.com (undated dealer page). — [ushopsmotors.co.il](http://ushopsmotors.co.il/our-services/carfax/) (secondary, low-medium confidence)
- "Carpax": the auto.co.il odometer-fraud article cites "Carpax data" showing a 14% rise in fraud in 2025. Its claim of 2.45 million vehicles with tampered mileage looks implausible for Israel's fleet, and may be European or global data. The relationship between Carpax and Carfax is unclear. — [auto.co.il used-car-mileage-fraud](https://www.auto.co.il/articles/car-news/industry/used-car-mileage-fraud/) (secondary; treat as suspect)
- AutoCheck: not researched or found.

### Inferences
- (my estimate) Prices sit at about 25–55 ₪ for private reports, while official sources charge 10–12 ₪ (clearing center) and 11 ₪ (lien). Much of the gov.il data is free and already wrapped by free apps. A new entrant's room for a premium price is narrow unless it bundles data that is hard to get, such as the lien check, the clearing report, listing history and photos, and an estimated price.
- (my estimate) The clearing center's owner-only access rule is a structural pain point. A buyer cannot pull accident history alone. Any product that makes this easier, such as a link the seller can share, would stand out.

## 5. How easily could incumbents copy, cut price or block a new entrant?

### Takeaway
(my estimate) Copying a better UI or cutting price is easy for Carzone, since its per-report cost on gov data is close to zero. Blocking access to government open data is not possible for incumbents. Their lasting advantages are a proprietary listings archive (Carzone, Yad2) and SEO footprint.

### Cited Findings
- The gov.il history datasets are public and free, and free apps such as Autoil already republish them. — [gov.il (1)](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/dynamiccollectors/private_vehicle_history_1), [Autoil](https://apps.apple.com/il/app/id6450055352) (secondary)
- Yad2's main asset is its marketplace of seller-reported prices. Levi Itzhak competes on "reliability" and attacks Yad2 publicly, which shows incumbents respond to rivals aggressively in the press. — [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1000613392) (secondary)

### Inferences
- (my estimate) The likely Carzone responses would be: a lower price or a bigger free preview; adding the clearing-center and lien checks to its bundle; anti-scraping on its listings archive. Its terms were not checked. Yad2 could add a plate report to its listings at almost no cost, which is the biggest threat.

### Gaps
- Carzone and Yad2 anti-scraping terms: not checked (fetch blocked).

## 6. User reviews and complaints

### Takeaway
There was little review data. Old carsforum threads are mostly positive on Carzone's detail, with skepticism about its value estimates. General market pain points: no single accident database, and owner-only access to the clearing report.

### Cited Findings
- Carsforum: Carzone "gives very accurate and useful information" and "really more details," such as past listings of the same car. One user questioned whether its estimated value is inflated.
